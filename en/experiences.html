Identification of mountain pastures using UAVs

2026

Science and technology go wherever the terrain demands. This time, at over 2,100 meters altitude in the Făgăraș Mountains, at the request of the Agency for Payments and Intervention for Agriculture (APIA).

The activity consisted of accompanying the control team and using drones to identify and map hard-to-reach mountain pastures. This experience directly demonstrates the current importance of specialized training, practical skills, and the integration of new technologies in the field.

Activity Highlights

  • Institutional support: Specialized technical assistance provided to APIA teams for field data validation.
  • Extreme operating environment: Flight missions executed in difficult mountain conditions at over 2,100 m altitude.
  • Applied technology: Precise identification of agricultural parcels using UAV equipment, overcoming topographical limitations.

Prediction and warning platform for potato late blight (Phytophthora infestans)

AOȘR Grant 2026-2027

Precision agriculture system based on a Neuro-Symbolic Architecture, developed for the Solanum tuberosum L. species.

The system separates mathematical rigor from synthesis capacity: it deterministically processes telemetry through validated epidemiological equations, subsequently using a restrictive Generative Artificial Intelligence engine to formulate recommendations in natural language, eliminating the risk of algorithmic "hallucinations".

Key Results & Features

  • Anticipation of critical infection windows calibrated through multispectral UAV remote sensing and visual phytopathological evaluations (Ground-Truth).
  • Dedicated dashboard with a digital phytosanitary register for integrated pest management (IPM).
  • Optimization of fungicide intervention windows and reduction of the chemical footprint on the soil.

Multi-Risk Prediction Platform for Malus domestica species

2026

The Multi-Risk Prediction Platform is a precision agriculture system based on a Neuro-Symbolic Architecture. It transforms raw microclimate and forecast data into proactive agronomic decisions, operating through a 5-level data pipeline.

The system separates mathematical rigor from synthesis capacity: it deterministically processes telemetry through scientifically validated models (Symbolic Level), and subsequently uses a Generative Artificial Intelligence engine (Cognitive Level) to formulate recommendations in natural language.

Key Results & Features

  • Anticipation of pathogen pressure, pest dynamics, and abiotic stress impact.
  • Constant calibration of predictions through a field validation loop (Ground-Truth).
  • Data distributed across 4 expertise modules to optimize interventions and reduce the chemical footprint.

Pre-screening platform for water stress phenotyping in potatoes

2025

Development and validation of a low-cost IoT phenotyping platform intended for pre-screening drought tolerance in potatoes.

The system integrates automated irrigation control, managed by an Arduino architecture with multiplexed capacitive sensors (6 sensors/line) and real-time microclimate monitoring via ESP8266.

Methodology & Validation

  • State machine algorithm for precise maintenance of humidity thresholds (60% optimal vs. 35% stress).
  • Reproducible induction of water stress and statistical discrimination of physiological responses between contrasting genotypes.
  • Robust and scalable methodological tool that optimizes the preliminary selection of varieties prior to agronomic field testing.

Implementation and testing of an automated drip irrigation system

2020 - Bachelor's Thesis

The thesis aimed at the design, implementation, and testing of an automated drip irrigation system for vegetable crops (lettuce and radishes).

Automation was achieved using an Arduino UNO microcontroller connected to soil moisture sensors. Water flow was optimized through an intelligent LM2596 step-down module.

Conclusions & Results

  • The system operated autonomously throughout the vegetation cycle, triggering the water pump via a relay exclusively when humidity dropped below the set threshold.
  • The efficiency of precision irrigation was validated by reducing water consumption and inhibiting weed development between rows.
  • An extreme hydrotropism phenomenon was recorded and discussed at the root system level.
